5. Source Verification

For every source, record:

* Title and author.
* Publication year or version.
* Relevant chapters or pages.
* Reliability and academic relevance.
* Date added.

6. Conflict Resolution

* Identify conflicting statements between sources.
* Prioritize current official course materials for course-specific requirements.
* Use current evidence-based references for clinical updates.
* Clearly explain unresolved differences.
* Never silently combine contradictory recommendations.

7. NotebookLM Integration

* Add verified learning documents to the relevant NotebookLM notebook.
* Keep original documents whenever possible.
* Use source citations when explaining medical knowledge.
* Record important source references in NOTES.md.
* Do not treat AI-generated summaries as original references.
