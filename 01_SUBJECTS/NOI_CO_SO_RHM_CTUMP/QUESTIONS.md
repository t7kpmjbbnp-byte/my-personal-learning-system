2. Competency-Based Assessment

Practice questions that assess the ability to apply knowledge, analyze information, solve problems, and make appropriate decisions.

A. Clinical Scenario-Based Questions

* Present a realistic clinical scenario.
* Identify important signs, symptoms, and risk factors.
* Determine the most appropriate interpretation or action.

B. Data Interpretation

* Interpret laboratory results.
* Analyze vital signs and clinical parameters.
* Identify abnormal findings.
* Connect data with possible diagnoses.

C. Clinical Reasoning

* Identify the key clinical problem.
* Generate and compare differential diagnoses.
* Select the most likely diagnosis.
* Explain the reasoning behind the decision.

D. Prioritization and Decision-Making

* Identify the most urgent problem.
* Choose the best next step.
* Recognize warning signs and emergency situations.
* Determine when referral or consultation is necessary.

E. Application and Transfer

* Apply knowledge to unfamiliar situations.
* Predict consequences of a clinical condition.
* Select appropriate management principles.
* Explain how a change in patient condition affects decisions.

F. Integrated Dentistry Questions

* Connect systemic diseases with oral health.
* Evaluate medical risks before dental procedures.
* Identify precautions for patients with systemic diseases.
* Recognize situations requiring medical consultation.

6. Difficulty Levels

* Easy: Recall and understanding.
* Medium: Application and interpretation.
* Hard: Analysis, integration, and decision-making.
* Advanced: Complex clinical scenarios requiring multiple competencies.

8. Question Quality Rules

1. Questions should be based on official course materials or reliable medical references.
2. Include reasoning and explanations, not just answer keys.
3. Explain why distractors are incorrect when relevant.
4. Include both knowledge-based and competency-based questions.
5. Use new situations to test genuine understanding.
6. Label difficulty and competency assessed.
7. Do not present generated questions as official CTUMP examination questions unless verified.
