## Status definitions

- `not_started`: identified but no capture attempted.
- `in_progress`: capture underway; items remain outstanding.
- `captured_unverified`: transferred but not checked against original/export.
- `checked`: compared with original/export; gaps documented.
- `partial_unavailable`: some relevant content could not be retrieved/exported.

## Inventory checklist

- [ ] Open the notebook's complete source list.
- [ ] Record every source exactly as named.
- [ ] Record source type and original URL/file location when available.
- [ ] Record publication/version/page details when available.
- [ ] Identify saved Notes and important chat sessions to preserve.
- [ ] Do not mark inventory complete until every visible source has an entry.

**Important:** A source register entry is provenance metadata, not a copy of the original file and not evidence that its medical content has been independently validated.
