Review Results

* Passed: Correctly recalled and explained without hints.
* Partial: Needed hints or made minor mistakes.
* Failed: Could not recall or explain correctly.

Review Rules

1. Test recall before showing the answer.
2. Ask questions without revealing hints initially.
3. Record the learner’s result.
4. Reschedule failed topics for earlier review.
5. Mark knowledge as retained only after successful independent recall.
