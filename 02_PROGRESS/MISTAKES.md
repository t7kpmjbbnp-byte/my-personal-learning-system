Mistake Categories

Correction Rules

1. Identify the exact mistake.
2. Explain why the mistake happened.
3. Ask the learner to correct it independently.
4. Give a similar question to test understanding.
5. Schedule a review if necessary.

Review Priority

* High: Repeated mistakes or fundamental misunderstandings.
* Medium: Mistakes that occur occasionally.
* Low: Minor mistakes corrected independently.
