COMPETENCY-BASED ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

1. Purpose

Evaluate genuine understanding, clinical reasoning, application, and decision-making rather than memorization alone.

2. Core Competencies

C1. Knowledge and Understanding

* Recall essential medical knowledge.
* Explain mechanisms and concepts.
* Distinguish similar conditions.

C2. Data Interpretation

* Interpret vital signs.
* Analyze laboratory results.
* Identify clinically significant abnormalities.

C3. Clinical Reasoning

* Identify key clinical problems.
* Generate differential diagnoses.
* Connect symptoms, signs, and investigations.

C4. Application

* Apply knowledge to unfamiliar scenarios.
* Predict clinical consequences.
* Select appropriate management principles.

C5. Prioritization and Safety

* Recognize emergencies and warning signs.
* Prioritize appropriate actions.
* Identify contraindications and risks.

C6. Dentistry Integration

* Relate systemic diseases to oral health.
* Assess medical risks before dental procedures.
* Identify when medical consultation is necessary.

3. Assessment Question Types

* Single-best-answer MCQs.
* Multiple-response questions.
* Clinical vignettes.
* Data and laboratory interpretation.
* Matching and classification.
* Sequential decision-making.
* Short-answer clinical reasoning.
* Integrated interdisciplinary cases.

4. Difficulty Levels

* Level 1: Recall.
* Level 2: Understanding.
* Level 3: Application.
* Level 4: Analysis.
* Level 5: Evaluation and integration.

5. Scoring Rubric

Score	Performance
0	No understanding
1	Recognizes basic information
2	Explains with guidance
3	Applies correctly
4	Analyzes independently
5	Integrates and justifies decisions

6. Tutor Assessment Rules

1. Ask one question at a time.
2. Do not reveal the answer before the learner responds.
3. Evaluate the reasoning, not only the final answer.
4. Provide hints before revealing explanations.
5. Explain why incorrect options are wrong.
6. Retest weak competencies using different scenarios.
7. Record recurring errors in MISTAKES.md.

7. Mastery Criteria

A competency is considered mastered when the learner:

* Demonstrates correct understanding.
* Applies knowledge independently.
* Solves at least two different assessment questions successfully.
* Recalls the concept later without prompting.

8. Assessment Integrity

* Clearly distinguish generated practice questions from official examination questions.
* Prioritize official course materials and lecturer-provided assessment criteria.
* Do not invent examination formats or claim predictions are certain.

9. Progress Tracking

Record competency levels in CURRENT_STATE.md and assessment outcomes in REVIEW_HISTORY.md.
